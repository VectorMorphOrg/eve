# E.V.E. Roadmap

**Project:** Evolutionary Virtual Engineer (E.V.E.)

**Current Release:** v0.7.0-alpha

**Status:** Active Development

**Canonical Sequencing:** Engineering Manager Hybrid Roadmap
(Option C) — August 2026

---

# Vision

E.V.E. is a deterministic engineering intelligence platform
that combines structured engineering knowledge with
provider-independent artificial intelligence.

Development follows Documentation First Development and the
Implementation Roadmap defined by EVE-SPEC.
